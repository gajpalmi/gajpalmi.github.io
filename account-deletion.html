GAMEZONE ARENA - Account Deletion

This page explains how GAMEZONE ARENA users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open your email account.
  2. Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your GAMEZONE ARENA account.
  3. Include your GAMEZONE ARENA username or registered email address.
  4. Write "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.
Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

  • GAMEZONE ARENA account/profile information.
  • Game-related account data associated with the user.
  • Other personal information associated with the account, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for security, fraud prevention, legal, regulatory, or other legitimate purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable laws and policies.

Important: Virtual coins and rewards in GAMEZONE ARENA have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
